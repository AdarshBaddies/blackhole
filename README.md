# Humanity Galaxy

A project building an infinite, 3D archive of human memories.

## Overview
**Humanity Galaxy** is an endless, zoomable digital universe designed to exist for decades. It serves as a modern, 3D successor to "limited" digital canvas concepts, aiming to create a continuous map of humanity that grows until it reaches a scale of one decillion individual photos.

## The Concept
This is a "digital time capsule" where every participant contributes to a permanent celestial structure. Unlike traditional websites, this project uses an infinite 3D coordinate system to ensure there is always room for the next memory.

## How it Works
1.  **Placement:** It costs **$1** to upload a single photo and secure its permanent position in the 3D space.
2.  **Persistence:** The project is designed with a "decade-first" mindset. Revenue is used to fund long-term storage and domain renewals to ensure the gallery remains accessible for future generations.
3.  **Community Driven:** This is a pure hobbyist experiment in digital legacy and large-scale data visualization.

## Vision
The ultimate goal is to reach a **decillion** ($10^{33}$) photos, creating a human-made universe as vast as the physical one. This is not just a website; it is an attempt to map the presence of every person who wishes to be remembered in the digital void.
